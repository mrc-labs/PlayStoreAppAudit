# Contributing to Play Store App Audit

Thanks for considering a contribution.

Play Store App Audit is developed in public under `GPL-3.0-only` and also
supports alternative commercial licensing by the project owner. The
contribution process is designed to preserve both models.

## Before opening a pull request

- Keep changes focused and avoid unrelated refactors.
- Do not include secrets, credentials, personal data or proprietary material.
- Do not copy code or assets unless their license is compatible and clearly
  identified.
- Keep platform-specific code isolated and preserve the shared Windows,
  macOS and Linux source tree.
- Run the project's compile, test, Ruff and relevant Qt smoke checks.
- Do not commit generated binaries or local build artifacts.

## Contributor License Agreement

Material code, documentation, test, build-system and design contributions
require acceptance of [CLA.md](CLA.md) before they can be merged.

Until CLA acceptance is automated, include this exact statement in the pull
request description:

`I have read and agree to the Play Store App Audit Contributor License Agreement (CLA.md) for this contribution.`

Very small corrections that are not copyright-significant may be accepted
without a CLA at the maintainer's discretion.

If an employer or another organisation may own rights in your work, make sure
you have authority to contribute it under the CLA.

## Issues and discussions

You do not need to sign the CLA merely to report a bug, suggest an idea or
participate in a discussion. The CLA applies when copyrightable material is
submitted for inclusion in the project.

## Licensing of accepted contributions

Accepted contributions remain owned by their contributors. They are included
in the public project under GPLv3 and, under the CLA grant, may also be used by
the project owner in alternative commercial distributions.

## Security and privacy

Do not publish credentials, private diagnostic bundles, device identifiers or
other sensitive information in issues or pull requests. Redact diagnostic data
before sharing it publicly.
